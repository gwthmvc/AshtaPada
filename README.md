# How to Play Ashtapada

Ashtapada is an ancient Indian race game that predates chess. The game is played on an 8x8 uncheckered grid, where players race their tokens based on the roll of cowrie shells.

## Components
* **The Board**: An 8x8 grid of 64 identical squares. Certain squares feature an "X" or a special design. These are called **castles** (safe spaces).
* **The Dice**: Traditionally, 4 or 6 cowrie shells are used. The number of shells that land mouth-up determines the move value.
* **The Pieces**: Two to four players each receive a set of distinct tokens (typically 2 to 4 tokens per player).

## Board Setup and Castles
The standard board has specific safe zones marked with an "X":
* The four corner squares.
* The four center squares.
* The two middle squares on each outer edge.

## The Objective
The goal is to move all of your tokens from your starting point, along a spiral path to the center of the board, and successfully exit them before your opponents do.

## Movement and Pathing
1. **Starting Point**: Players enter the board from one of the outer edge castles.
2. **The Path**: Tokens travel in a anti-clockwise outer loop around the perimeter of the board, spiraling inward clockwise toward the center squares.
3. **Dice Values**: Roll the cowrie shells to determine your movement points. If using 4 cowrie shells:
    * 1 mouth up = 1 space
    * 2 mouth up = 2 spaces
    * 3 mouth up = 3 spaces
    * 4 mouth up = 4 spaces (plus an extra turn)
    * 0 mouth up (all upside down) = 8 spaces (plus an extra turn)

## Rules of Play
* **Entering Pieces**: A player must roll a specific value (often an 8 or 4 depending on local variations) to enter their first piece onto their starting castle.
* **Capturing**: If a token lands on a square occupied by an opponent's token, the opponent's token is captured and sent back to the starting pool.
* **Safe Zones (Castles)**: Tokens resting on a marked castle square cannot be captured. Multiple tokens, even from opposing players, can safely occupy the same castle square.
* **Doubling Up**: If you land two of your own tokens on the same non-castle square, they form a "twin." Twin tokens can move together and cannot be captured by a single token.

## Winning the Game
Once a token reaches the central squares, it must make an exact roll to exit the board. The first player to successfully navigate all of their tokens to the center and exit them wins the game.
